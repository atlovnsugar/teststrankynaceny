WHAT THE MAP MEASURES

Observed flow vs. infrastructure

Gas: ENTSOG physical-flow observations are attached to transmission points. The map shows actual interconnection points and the historical series returned by ENTSOG. Pipeline route geometry is a separate infrastructure layer.

Oil: Eurostat can show import volumes by ultimate origin; it does not provide a complete EU-wide pipeline-throughput series for every oil pipeline. Therefore the application never turns an import trade volume into a fake pipeline volume.

PIPELINE SOURCES

Route data provenance

Current gas and oil route snapshots are refreshed from Global Energy Monitor’s public pipeline-map data. Their tracker geometry is used as infrastructure metadata; route lines are not treated as measured flow.

For gas, ENTSOG remains the source of observed transmission volumes. For historical oil flows, the application separates trade-origin volumes from pipeline infrastructure.
