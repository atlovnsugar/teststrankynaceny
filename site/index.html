METHOD / PROVENANCE / OSINT

Sources, refresh and limits

The application separates primary statistical series from the secondary Czech regional layer and never fabricates a missing observation.

OPERATIONS

How the data stays current

EU fuels: every refresh reads the European Commission's full "Price developments 2005 onwards" workbook, rebuilds the weekly national history, then exposes the newest week as the current snapshot. The Commission states that its Weekly Oil Bulletin contains weekly consumer prices for petroleum products across EU countries.

Natural gas: the refresh reads Eurostat nrg_pc_202, selecting D2, EUR/kWh and I_TAX. The interface keeps the full half-year series rather than only the latest semester.

CZK conversion: the app does not use a hard-coded exchange rate. It imports the ECB's daily EUR/CZK and USD/CZK reference-rate history and uses the rate at, or immediately before, each observation date.

Czech regions: the public regional source reports averages by Czech region and cites Czech Statistical Office data. Its history is retained as dated snapshots when available; if the feed fails, the previous known-good snapshot stays on screen.

Europe map: NUTS 2024 country geometry is filtered to the European geographic theater before publication, removing overseas departments and distant non-European EU territories from the overview map while retaining Mediterranean EU member states.

Deployment: GitHub Pages serves the static site; GitHub Actions performs the weekly ETL and commits refreshed data. There is no database or paid backend.
